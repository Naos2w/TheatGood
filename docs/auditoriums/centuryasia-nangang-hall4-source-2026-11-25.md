Observed on 2026-11-25 via the operator's logged-out public seat preview, selecting two screenings on 2026-11-26: [11:00](https://www.centuryasia.com.tw/Movie/GetSeatView/Nangang/0001221/2026-11-26/11-00/0004) and [15:25](https://www.centuryasia.com.tw/Movie/GetSeatView/Nangang/0001221/2026-11-26/15-25/0004). Both returned HTTP 200 and identical row labels, numbered seat coordinates and wheelchair marker. The displayed DOM confirms four rows, ten internal columns after excluding the two side row-label cells, and the empty top padding row is excluded. The first internal column is blank in every row; the display reverses source arrayX (column = 10 - arrayX). Ordinary seat counts per row are 6, 8, 8, 9; the wheelchair marker is row 1 column 9. The 31 numbered seats and one wheelchair marker reconcile with the [published capacity of 32](https://www.centuryasia.com.tw/cinemaQA.html). The operator's screen dimensions are unknown, so 3D remains estimated. No booking, selection, artwork, availability or session data is retained.
